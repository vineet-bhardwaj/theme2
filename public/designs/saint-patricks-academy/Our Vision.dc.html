Our vision, and the work
it asks of us.

A vision is only worth stating if it changes what a school actually does. Here is ours — followed by the mission, the values and the commitments we hold ourselves to in front of every parent who trusts us with a child.

{{ st.num }} {{ st.label }}
Our Vision
To be a school where every child discovers their own strengths, and grows into a confident, compassionate citizen ready to serve a changing world.

Two things in that sentence are deliberate. Every child — not the top set. And their own strengths — not the ones a rank list happens to reward.

Our Mission

The vision, turned into a working brief

To deliver a values-led, inquiry-based education that balances academic rigour with character, creativity and care for the community — and to do it for every child on the roll, not a favoured few.

How we know it is working

Every commitment on this page is reviewed by the school board each year, and reported to parents at the annual general meeting. A promise no one audits is only decoration.
