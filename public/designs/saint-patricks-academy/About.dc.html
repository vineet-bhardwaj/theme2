Our Story

Six decades of shaping
confident, compassionate minds.

Founded in 1965 by the Patrician Brothers, St. Patrick's Academy has grown into one of Meerut's most respected schools — blending timeless values with a future-ready education on a green twelve-acre campus.

Heritage & History

A legacy built on faith, learning and service

What began in 1965 as a modest schoolhouse with a handful of pupils has, over six decades, become a thriving community of more than two thousand learners. Through every era, our purpose has stayed the same — to form young people of character, curiosity and conscience.

Guided by the Patrician tradition and our motto “For God and Country,” we pair academic rigour with the arts, sport and service, so that every child leaves us ready for the world beyond the school gate.
