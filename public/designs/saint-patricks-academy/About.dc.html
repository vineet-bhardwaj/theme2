St. Patrick's Academy Meerut

The sister concern of St. Mary’s Academy, Meerut Cantt.; is another fruit of the Patrician educational endeavours in India.

A legacy built on faith, learning and service

The sister concern of St. Mary’s Academy, Meerut Cantt.; is another fruit of the Patrician educational endeavours in India. The idea of having another prestigious educational concern in or around Meerut of the same tenor of St. Mary’s Academy was born out of a collective desire and the demands of the people of Meerut at large. In 2012, the community at St. Mary’s Academy gave shape to this idea and set about to realise it. After obtaining necessary consensus from the Indian Province’s consensus team, the community began the ground work on a very positive note. On 16th June 2012, the foundation stone was laid by Rev. Bro. Bosco, the Indian Province Leader of Patrician Brothers.
Much credit goes to Bro. Babu Varghese and Bro. George P.U. for their earnest effort in turning this dream into a reality. Classes for the first session began in the auditorium at St. Mary’s Academy in 2014. It was only in 2015 that St. Patrick’s Academy began classes in its new campus. And since then, there has been no looking back. With each passing year, the school has only progressed and established new standards for itself to achieve.
